About

This Electronic Press Kit presents a selection of my work as a UK-based producer and vocalist, showcasing my development over the past year. The material included reflects a progression in sound, approach, and confidence, moving from early ideas and initial sketches to more refined and intentional productions.

Over the course of this period, my growth has been shaped through consistent collaboration with a close friend, as well as guidance and feedback from more experienced producers and industry professionals. These experiences have played a key role in refining my production skills, broadening my understanding of arrangement and sound design, and helping me develop a clearer artistic direction.

The music featured below represents a contrast between earlier work and more recent output, highlighting how my sound has evolved within just a few months. I’m always open to new collaborations, feedback, and opportunities, and I welcome anyone interested in working together or learning more about my work to get in touch.
