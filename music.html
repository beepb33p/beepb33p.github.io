A collection of works in progress and live performance clips, capturing different stages of my creative process. From unfinished productions to vocal-led performances, this page shows how my sound and performance style are evolving.

I don’t stick to one fixed style or process, and I tend to follow whatever idea feels right at the time. Some ideas come from melodies, others from casual moments with friends where we’re just adlibbing and experimenting, and I’ll take whatever stands out and build on it. The tracks often develop in different ways from there, depending on the mood or sound I want to explore.

My sound is still evolving, and I’m using this space to experiment, learn, and capture moments as they come. Some tracks are emotional, some are raw, and some are just ideas that felt worth saving in the moment.
